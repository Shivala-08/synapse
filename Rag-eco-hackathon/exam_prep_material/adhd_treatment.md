# ADHD Treatment Approaches

## Pharmacological Treatments

### Stimulant Medications

#### Methylphenidate (Ritalin, Concerta)
- **Mechanism**: Blocks dopamine transporter (DAT) and norepinephrine transporter (NET)
- **Onset**: 20-30 minutes (immediate release), 1-2 hours (extended release)
- **Duration**: 3-4 hours (IR), 8-12 hours (ER)
- **Efficacy**: 70-80% response rate in ADHD
- **Side effects**: Appetite suppression, insomnia, growth suppression, tics

#### Amphetamine (Adderall, Vyvanse)
- **Mechanism**: Increases dopamine and norepinephrine release, blocks reuptake
- **Onset**: 30-60 minutes
- **Duration**: 4-6 hours (IR), 10-14 hours (ER)
- **Efficacy**: 70-85% response rate
- **Side effects**: Similar to methylphenidate, plus potential for abuse

#### Lisdexamfetamine (Vyvanse)
- **Unique**: Prodrug requiring enzymatic conversion in body
- **Advantage**: Lower abuse potential, smoother effect curve
- **Duration**: 10-14 hours

### Non-Stimulant Medications

#### Atomoxetine (Strattera)
- **Mechanism**: Selective norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (NRI)
- **Onset**: 2-4 weeks for full effect
- **Advantage**: No abuse potential, 24-hour coverage
- **Efficacy**: 40-60% response rate
- **Side effects**: Nausea, fatigue, mood changes

#### Guanfacine (Intuniv) and Clonidine (Kapvay)
- **Mechanism**: Alpha-2 adrenergic agonists
- **Primary use**: Hyperactivity and impulsivity
- **Advantage**: Can be combined with stimulants
- **Side effects**: Sedation, hypotension

## Behavioral Treatments

### Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
- Addresses negative thought patterns
- Develops coping strategies
- Improves organizational skills
- Effective for adults with ADHD

### Parent Training in Behavior Management
- Positive reinforcement techniques
- Consistent discipline strategies
- Communication skills
- Homework routines

### Organizational Skills Training
- Time management techniques
- Task prioritization
- Note-taking systems
- Digital organization tools

### Psychoeducation
- Understanding ADHD neurobiology
- Self-advocacy skills
- Academic accommodations
- Workplace strategies

## Lifestyle Interventions

### Exercise
- Increases dopamine and norepinephrine
- Improves executive function
- Reduces hyperactivity
- Recommended: 30+ minutes daily

### Sleep Hygiene
- Consistent sleep schedule
- Screen time limitations
- Relaxation techniques
- Environmental modifications

### Nutrition
- Regular meal timing
- Protein-rich breakfast
- Omega-3 fatty acids
- Limited processed foods

## Key Takeaways
- Stimulants are first-line treatment with 70-85% efficacy
- Non-stimulants offer alternatives for specific populations
- Behavioral interventions complement medication
- Lifestyle factors significantly impact ADHD symptoms
- Treatment often requires multimodal approach
